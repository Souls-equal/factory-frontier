FIELD OPERATIONS

Construction

Collect branches and stones or chop trees with E. The Miner needs 10 stone and 10 wood. Select a building, aim at nearby ground, rotate with R and place repeatedly with left click; drag to look or right click to capture the mouse when unlocked; Esc exits placement. For foundations, Page Up and Page Down adjust height by 0.1 m; neighbouring foundations align and share their level; near a raised lift port or flat conveyor, the foundation aligns to support that level. For lifts, Page Up and Page Down change height by 1 m; hold Shift for 0.1 m steps, from 1 to 8 m before placement. Aligned lift ports can connect directly to compatible machines, storage, splitters, mergers or other lifts at the same height. Each conveyor fits one grid cell and faces your view; R rotates it. Yellow marks the output. Aim the output at the next belt to transfer items, even if it faces another direction. Items fall from an open end and can be picked up with E. Open a splitter with E to choose its distribution mode, including item filters for its three outputs. Aim at a conveyor or lift and press G to inspect its flow.
